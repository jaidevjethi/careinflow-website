# 2 · Non-negotiables — what can NEVER appear on a graphic

These are integrity rules, not style preferences. Breaking one is worse than
shipping nothing.

### Banned outright — fabricated facts

- ❌ Star ratings, review counts, "4.9★", "rated best"
- ❌ Client counts — "50+ clinics", "trusted by 100 doctors", "200 projects"
- ❌ Awards, badges, certifications the studio does not hold
- ❌ Testimonials from people who did not say them
- ❌ Any promise about **outcomes**: "we'll get you to #1", "3× more patients",
  "guaranteed rankings", "+250% traffic"
- ❌ Fake statistics or made-up percentages of any kind

### Banned — scarcity and discounting

- ❌ "Limited slots", "only 3 spots left", "founding practice offer"
- ❌ Countdowns, deadlines, "offer ends Friday", "book before…"
- ❌ Discounts, "50% off", "special price", "was ₹X now ₹Y"
- ❌ "Buy now", "Hurry", "Don't miss out"

Premium positioning does not discount. Where a combined plan costs less than
its parts, the reason stated is *the work overlaps* — never *a discount*.

### Banned — tone

No buzzwords. No "solutions", "cutting-edge", "digital ecosystems", "synergy",
"revolutionary", "game-changing", "one-stop shop", "why choose us".

### The one exception — opinion is allowed

Superlatives **about ourselves** are fine, because they are opinion and not
falsifiable. These are approved and in use on the site:

- ✅ "Gujarat's best healthcare websites"
- ✅ "Gujarat's healthcare-only web studio"
- ✅ "Clinics are the only thing we build for, which is why we build it better"

Place them, do not spray them. One per asset, in the headline.

### Client facts you must state correctly

CareInflow has **exactly two real clients**, both in Mehsana:

1. **Pramukh Multispeciality Dental Clinic** — website + Google Business Profile
   (pramukhdentalclinic.com). The only live build for a paying practice.

The other three sites in the portfolio — **Lavanya Skin Studio**, **Gati

# 3 · Voice — how the words on a graphic should sound

Written for a **busy doctor reading on a phone**, often in their second
language. Clarity over cleverness, always.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences. Plain English. | Long clauses, jargon, marketing-speak. |
| Answer the question the reader has. | Describe how great the studio is. |
| State a mechanism. | Promise a result. |
| "We build modern websites that help your clinic look professional, earn patient trust, and perform well on Google." | "We create cutting-edge digital ecosystems." |

**Brand personality:** professional, modern, reliable, calm, helpful, honest,
detail-oriented. Friendly without being casual. Confident without being
arrogant.

**Never sounds:** corporate, flashy, trendy, sales-driven, overly technical.

### Approved headline patterns

These are live on the site and safe to reuse verbatim on graphics.

- "Gujarat's best healthcare websites. Found, trusted, booked."
- "Our prices are published, so you do not have to ask."
- "We do not guarantee rankings, and nobody honestly can."
- "Building the site is the easy part. Deciding what goes on it is not."
- "The price we agree is the price that holds."
- "Every project starts with a look at your Google listing."
- "Patients do not search 'endodontics'. They search the thing that hurts."
- "Most clinic websites describe the clinic. Yours has to answer what the
  patient arrived worrying about."
- "A patient who has never met you decides in about a minute whether your
  clinic looks trustworthy."

### Approved CTA wording

- "Message us on WhatsApp"
- "Get a free written review"
- "See what it costs"
- "Ask us anything on WhatsApp"

Never: "Buy now", "Get started today", "Claim your offer", "Limited time".

## 11.2 · The colour rules that hold it together

1. **One cool family.** Every neutral is a desaturated tint of the brand blue.
   Every neutral measures b\* ≤ 0. **Positive b\* is the yellow axis — that is
   beige, and beige is banned.** No cream, no warm greys, no sand.
2. **Midnight is for moments** — header, hero, CTA panel, footer, and at most
   two sections per page. Never a dark theme. A dark card in a light section
   takes the eye first, so spend that weight on what you want clicked.
3. **No gradients, no glass, no glow.** Solid colour-blocked sections and
   saturated small elements. Crisp edges are the point.
4. **Green is reserved.** Only `pass` (verified measurements) and the two
   WhatsApp tokens. No other green, and nothing in the sage band (hue 150–205).
5. **Vivid indigo takes ink text, never white.**
6. **Large tight numbers in their accent colour are the only ornament.**

## 11.3 · Type

| Role | Font | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Manrope Variable** | Weight 800, tracking −0.045em (headings −0.03em, hero −0.05em), line-height ~1.02 |
| Body | **Manrope Variable** | 18px / 1.62 |
| Labels, chips, stats captions | **JetBrains Mono Variable** | ~11px, uppercase, tracking 0.08–0.1em |

Manrope only for everything except mono labels. No third typeface.

## 11.4 · Layout & shape

- Max width **1440px**, gutters 20 / 40 / **80px**
- Section rhythm **88px** (60px on small screens)
- Radius **12 / 16 / 24px** — cards are `rounded-3xl` (24px)
- Exactly **one shadow recipe**:
  `0 1px 2px rgba(14,36,57,.05), 0 16px 36px rgba(14,36,57,.06)`
- Grids must divide evenly. Nothing crosses the viewport at 320px or 1440px.
- Cards: 1px `line` border on `surface`, with a 4px accent bar across the top in
  the service's `-vivid` colour.

## 11.5 · Motion (for video/reels)

Single easing `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)`. Entrance = 240ms fade + 8px rise,
**once**. Hover = 200ms lift. Only opacity and transform ever animate. Respect
reduced motion.

## 11.6 · Section labels — plain language

Approved: "What we do" · "Where patients look" · "What it costs" · "Questions" ·
"Method". Set in mono, above a tick-rule.

Retired and never to be used again: CHART, TRIAGE, PROTOCOL, "REF <1.2S"
ranges, "Solutions", "Why choose us".
