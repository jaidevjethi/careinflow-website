CareInflow · design system · three directions

Three design systems that prove the expertise instead of describing it.

Built like software rather than print: real interface artifacts on the page, so a doctor can see your craft in the Google listing you designed, the search result you wrote, the AI answer you engineered and the load time you measured. No gradients, no glass, no motion tricks — the modernity comes from precision, scale and surface quality.

Compare 2a, 2b and 2c.

Product-grade and bright. Large soft-edged surfaces, one crisp blue, tight geometric type, and live-looking interface artifacts embedded in the page. The homepage is organised around the three surfaces a patient actually meets — Google listing, website, AI answer — each shown as a designed component rather than described in a paragraph.

Rules
12 columns, 1440 max, 80px margin, 8px baseline, 140–160px section rhythm. Radius 12 / 16 / 24. Exactly one shadow recipe. Motion: 200ms opacity plus 8px rise on entry, and a 1px border darken on hover. Nothing parallaxes, nothing counts up, nothing autoplays.
