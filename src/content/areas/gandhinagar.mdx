## A city that is searched differently

Gandhinagar is addressed by sector, and patients search it that way. Sector 21, Sector 7, Kudasan, Infocity, Sargasan. Not landmarks, not neighbourhood names, numbers.

That sounds like a small thing and it is not, because the sector is the single detail most likely to be inconsistent across a clinic's online presence. The website says one sector, an old directory entry says another, the Google listing has the plot number but not the sector, and a third-party health portal has an address from two premises ago.

Google cross-checks all of it. When those records disagree, the map result gets weaker, and the map result is where most patients here start.

Getting this right costs nothing at the outset and is tedious to unpick afterwards. It is the first thing we look at for a Gandhinagar practice.

## Hours matter more here than in most places

A large share of patients in Gandhinagar work government and institutional hours, which means they visit in a narrow window: early morning, lunch, or after five.

Two consequences follow.

Your published hours have to be exactly right, including festival closures and the days a particular doctor is not in. A patient who plans an evening visit around a listing that says 7pm and arrives to a closed door does not usually try again.

And the enquiry path matters more than the phone number. Someone deciding at 4pm whether to come at 6 will message from their desk rather than call. A WhatsApp enquiry that arrives already carrying which page they were reading gets your front desk further than a missed call ever does.

## Competing with Ahmedabad rather than against it

Gandhinagar patients compare against Ahmedabad clinics as a matter of course. It is close, it is larger, and it has more of everything.

Trying to out-compete Ahmedabad on its own terms is a poor use of a Gandhinagar practice's money. The honest advantage is distance: the same standard of care, twenty minutes instead of an hour each way, with parking.

So the site's job is to make that obvious. Accurate location, clear directions, honest waiting times, rather than shouting louder than a market with more clinics in it. Patients who are already weighing a longer journey do not need persuading that closer is better. They need reassuring that closer is not worse.

## What the groundwork looks like here

Lighter competition than Ahmedabad means the ordinary work still moves things. A page for each treatment you offer, written to what patients actually search. A listing that is claimed, complete, correct and maintained. Consistent details everywhere they appear. Reviews answered, drafted for your approval, never posted blind.

None of that is clever. It is simply done, and kept done, which is where most practices come unstuck.
