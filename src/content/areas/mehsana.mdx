## Where we are

Our studio is in Mehsana, on Radhanpur Road. It is the only one we have: no branches, no second office, no partner firm in another city presented as ours.

That is worth stating plainly, because a lot of agencies list a dozen cities on their website and staff exactly one of them. We serve practices across North Gujarat and further; we sit here.

## What being here actually changes

Honestly, less than you would expect, and one thing more than you would expect.

The writing, the build, the Google listing work, the monthly upkeep: none of it improves by being in the same town. We would be overselling the point to suggest otherwise.

What does change is photography. A clinic website lives or dies on whether the pictures look like the practice a patient will walk into, and stock images are visible from a distance. Being in Mehsana means we can come and look at your reception, your chairs, the light in the afternoon, and plan a proper shot list for your photographer, or shoot the reference frames ourselves.

For practices further away we still plan the shot list, just from a call. It works. It is simply better in person.

## What the Mehsana market looks like

Patients here search on phones, on mobile data, mostly on mid-range Android handsets. That is not a caveat, it is the primary case, and it decides how the site gets built. Our reference is a page that opens in under 1.2 seconds on that phone on 4G, measured before launch rather than assumed.

Language is the other local decision. A meaningful share of patients read comfortably in Gujarati, and for many practices here a properly written Gujarati version earns its cost. For others it does not, where the patient base is younger, more urban and more English-first. We will tell you which after looking at your practice rather than selling the second version by default.

The third thing worth knowing is that fewer clinics here compete properly online than in Ahmedabad. Listings are half-filled, hours are wrong, nobody replies to reviews. That means the ordinary groundwork goes further here than it would in a harder market, and that advantage closes over time.

## Patients travel further than clinics assume

Mehsana practices draw from Visnagar, Unjha, Kadi, Kheralu and the villages around them. Patients who will drive forty minutes for the right specialist search differently from patients choosing between two clinics on the same road, they search the treatment first and the town second.

That is why the area pages we build are for the towns a practice genuinely serves, written about that town, rather than the same paragraph with the name swapped. A page that says nothing specific about Unjha does not help anyone in Unjha find you.
