## Why do patients travel to Palanpur for care?

Palanpur is the headquarters of Banaskantha, and for a very large rural catchment it is where specialist care begins.

That gives a practice here a particular kind of patient: one who is not local, who is travelling a real distance on rural roads, and who is trying to work out, before setting off, whether the journey is necessary at all.

Answer that question well and you get a patient who arrives prepared. Leave it unanswered and you get a phone call you cannot always take, and a patient who tries somewhere else while they wait.

## What should a Palanpur clinic website say first?

The most valuable page on a Palanpur clinic's website is usually the least glamorous one: a straight account of what you handle, what you refer on, whether a referral is needed to see you, whether patients are seen the same day, and what a first visit actually involves.

Practices resist this, because listing what you do not do feels like turning work away. In a referral market it does the opposite. It means the patients who arrive are the ones you can help, and the ones you cannot were never going to become appointments, only disappointments.

## What happens when a referred patient searches your name?

A large share of new patients here are sent by a smaller clinic somewhere else in the district. The referral is verbal. The patient is given a name.

Then they search that name, and what loads is either a practice that plainly exists and is plainly competent, or a bare listing with no photographs and hours nobody has updated since it was created.

An incomplete listing sitting under a strong recommendation is among the most expensive small problems a referral practice can have. It is also among the cheapest to fix, which is why we start there.

## Why does website speed matter on weak rural signal?

Much of this catchment is on weak mobile signal. A site built the usual way, with heavy images, a stack of scripts and fonts loading last, can take many seconds to appear on that kind of connection, and almost nobody waits.

We build to open in under 1.2 seconds on a mid-range Android over mobile data, and we measure it there rather than on a fast office connection, because the office connection is not the one your patients are using.

## How competitive is clinic search in Palanpur?

Competition here is moderate but unusually uneven. A handful of practices are genuinely well presented online and most are not, which means the gap between the top result and everything below it is wider than the gap in actual clinical quality.
