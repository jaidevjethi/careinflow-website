## Where do a Patan clinic’s patients actually come from?

Patan is the headquarters of its district, and that shapes everything about how a practice here is found.

A meaningful share of your appointments come from villages and smaller towns spread across the district. Those patients are not choosing between you and the clinic down the road. They are deciding whether to spend half a day travelling at all.

That is a different question, and it is answered before anyone reaches your door.

## Why do wrong opening hours cost a Patan clinic more?

Someone who lives ten minutes away and finds your hours wrong is mildly annoyed. Someone who travels forty kilometres and finds them wrong has lost a day, and will not repeat the experiment.

So on a Patan listing, the unglamorous fields carry more weight than they do almost anywhere else we work: opening hours including festival closures, which days a particular doctor is actually in, the building as it looks from the road, and a phone number that someone answers.

We treat those as the first job, not the last one. They are also, conveniently, free to get right.

## How do visitors and families elsewhere choose a clinic?

Patan has steady visitor traffic, and a great many families with a parent still living here and a son or daughter working in Ahmedabad or further away.

Neither group can judge your practice the way a neighbour can. They have never seen your reception, never met your staff, and know nobody who has. Everything they conclude about you comes from what Google shows them: photographs, hours, the reviews, and whether anyone from the practice ever replies to one.

This is the group most clinics in Patan lose without ever knowing they were in the running. It is also the easiest to win, because the work is entirely within your control.

## What do we build differently for a Patan clinic?

We build the site around the journey rather than around the practice. That means a page for each treatment written in the words a patient uses, directions and landmarks written for someone who does not know the town, and an enquiry path that opens WhatsApp with the message already written, because a patient forty kilometres away would far rather message than call.

Then we keep the listing correct, month after month, which is where nearly every practice comes unstuck.

None of it is clever. It is simply done, and kept done.

## How competitive is clinic search in Patan?

Competition here is lighter than in Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar, and most practices have a listing that is unclaimed, incomplete or three years out of date. That is an opportunity with a shelf life: the ordinary groundwork still moves you past them, and it will not always.
