## Who actually chooses a clinic in Siddhpur?

Siddhpur is a small town with an unusually wide address book. Its families are spread across India and well beyond it, and that changes who is actually making decisions about care here.

Often it is not the patient. It is a son or daughter living in Ahmedabad, Mumbai, the Gulf or further, arranging treatment for a parent who still lives in the town.

That person cannot drop in to see your reception. They cannot ask a neighbour. They have never met your staff and never will before the appointment.

## What does someone abroad judge your clinic on?

Everything they conclude about your practice comes off a phone screen, which puts unusual weight on a short list of things:

Photographs of a practice that visibly exists, and of the actual building, so a parent can be told where to go. Hours that are right. Reviews with replies under them, because a reply is the only evidence a stranger has that someone at the practice is paying attention. And a clear account of what a treatment involves, because they are explaining it to a parent over the phone before they book it.

None of that is persuasion. It is simply making it possible for someone to choose you from a distance.

## How should a Siddhpur clinic handle enquiries from abroad?

This is the practical failure we see most in Siddhpur.

An enquiry from someone six hours out of step with you cannot be a phone call. If your website offers a number and nothing else, that enquiry either does not happen or goes to whichever clinic made messaging possible.

So the enquiry path here is built around a message rather than a call, and the message arrives already carrying the treatment page the sender was reading. You answer it when you open. Nobody has to be awake at the same time, and nothing is lost in the gap.

## Does being nearby matter in Siddhpur?

Siddhpur is compact. Almost nowhere is far from anywhere, so being nearby is not a reason to choose you, and a site that leans on convenience is arguing something the town does not care about.

What works instead is specificity: a page for each treatment, written plainly, that lets someone at a distance understand exactly what you do and what it will involve. That is the substitute for walking in and having a look.

## What does the groundwork involve for a Siddhpur clinic?

Claim and complete the listing, with real photographs of the building. Set the hours correctly and keep them that way. Answer every review, drafted for your approval. A page per treatment in the words patients use. One consistent set of details everywhere they appear.

Competition online here is very light, and almost no practice in the town is set up for the enquiry that comes from outside it — which is the enquiry most worth having.
