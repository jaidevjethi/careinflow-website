## Which patients does a Visnagar clinic actually get?

Visnagar has a long metalworking tradition and a substantial student population. Between them they hand the town's clinics two caseloads that most practices here have never deliberately built for.

Skilled manual work produces predictable injuries: hands, shoulders, backs, eyes, burns. And a large student population lives here away from home, with no family doctor, no local relatives, and no one to ask.

Neither group behaves like the patient a clinic website is usually written for.

## What do Visnagar patients type into Google?

This is the thing that decides who gets found in this town.

A worker whose shoulder has been getting worse for a month does not type "orthopaedic clinic". A student with a rash does not type "dermatology". They type what hurts, in ordinary words, and Google sends them to whichever practice has a page about that.

A single "Services" list, the page nearly every clinic here has, cannot answer any of those searches. A page per treatment, written in the words patients actually use, answers all of them.

That is the entire mechanism, and in Visnagar it is close to uncontested.

## What does an injured worker need to know first?

A skilled worker with an injured hand has one urgent question: how soon can I work again.

Practices tend to answer that with "call us". The practice that answers it on the page, saying what the treatment involves, how quickly you can be seen and what recovery usually looks like, is the one that gets the visit, because uncertainty is the expensive part for that patient.

Naming work-related injuries specifically is worth doing here in a way it would not be in a city: hand injuries, repetitive strain, eye injuries, burns, each written plainly.

## How do students choose a clinic?

Students choose from a phone, fast, on whatever the connection happens to be. What decides it is a short list: do you look open, do the reviews have replies under them, and can they message instead of calling.

The last one matters more than practices expect. A nineteen-year-old will not phone a clinic they have never visited. They will message, or they will pick a different one.

So the enquiry path is a WhatsApp message that arrives already carrying the page they were reading, and the site is built to load in under 1.2 seconds on a mid-range Android over mobile data, which is the phone and the connection this group actually has.

## How competitive is clinic search in Visnagar?

Competition here is light and largely unaware. Most practices have no treatment pages at all, which leaves the symptom searches, the ones both of these groups make, sitting uncontested.
