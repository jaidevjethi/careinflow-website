## Why this project is here

Divyam Tours is not a healthcare practice, and we will not pretend
otherwise. It is included because it shows the same method we bring to
clinics, applied end to end on a different local business — and because the
patterns transfer directly.

## What we built

A search-focused website for a Varanasi tours and taxi operator, where
nearly every customer arrives from Google.

The structure is the point:

**A page per service.** Taxi services, airport transfer, local
sightseeing — each has its own dedicated page targeting what people
actually search for, instead of one thin page trying to rank for
everything. This is exactly the treatment-page discipline we apply to
[clinic websites](/services/healthcare-websites).

**Structured data from day one.** LocalBusiness JSON-LD ships in the
site's base layout, so every page tells search engines precisely what the
business is, where it operates, and how to contact it.

**An enquiry flow that never disappears.** A persistent call-to-action
follows the visitor on every page — because a customer ready to book
should never have to hunt for the button.

## The transferable lesson

Local businesses win search the same way: real pages for real searches,
consistent structured details, and an effortless next step. For a clinic,
swap "airport transfer" for "root canal treatment" and the discipline is
identical — only the care taken with the reader changes, because a patient
reads differently than a tourist.
