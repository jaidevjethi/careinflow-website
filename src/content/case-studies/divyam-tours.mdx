## Why this project is on a healthcare site

Divyam Tours is not a clinic, and we are not going to pretend the work is
something it is not. It is here because it is a complete, live example of
the same method, and because a practice owner deciding whether to trust us
deserves to see everything we have shipped rather than a curated half.

The patterns transfer almost exactly. Where this site has a page for
"airport transfer", a clinic has a page for "root canal treatment". The
discipline is identical. What changes is the care taken with the reader,
because a worried patient reads very differently from somebody booking a
car.

## Honest scope

We built and shipped the website. We do not manage this business's Google
listing, we do not run its ads, and we are not going to attribute its
