## The practice

Pramukh Multispeciality Dental Clinic serves patients at Radhanpur Circle
in Mehsana. Like most good clinics, its reputation was built in person —
the website needed to carry that same impression to patients who check
online first.

## What we built

A complete marketing website with a page for every one of the clinic's 11
treatments, plus the pages patients actually look for: about, gallery,
patient information, areas served, and contact.

Two decisions shaped the build:

**Bilingual by design.** Many of the clinic's patients are more comfortable
in Gujarati. The entire site — every heading, every treatment explanation —
works in both English and Gujarati with a single toggle, driven by one
translation dictionary so the two languages never drift apart.

**Fully static.** The site ships plain HTML and CSS with no framework
JavaScript at all. There is simply nothing to slow it down on a mid-range
phone, and nothing to break. Images are optimized WebP, capped at 1600px.

## The details that matter

- Scroll animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — patients who disable
  animations get an instant, still page.
- A gallery lightbox handles before/after treatment photos properly.
- A sitemap, robots file, and per-page metadata were part of launch, not an
  afterthought.

## Honest scope

This project covers the website surface. It is live at
[pramukhdentalclinic.com](https://pramukhdentalclinic.com) — open it on
your own phone and judge the loading speed yourself.
