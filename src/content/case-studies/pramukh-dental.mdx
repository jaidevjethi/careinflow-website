## What were we asked to build?

A website that could carry a first impression the practice had never had to
make in writing before.

The framing we settled on decided everything after it: this is not a
brochure for people who already chose the clinic, it is built for the
patient who has not, who is deciding between three options on a phone and
knows none of them. Every structural choice below follows from that one
sentence.

It also set what we did *not* build. No welcome message, no mission
statement, no photograph of the building on the homepage, because none of
those answer the question the visitor arrived with. Dr. Chinmay Patel's
credentials, an MDS root canal specialist with more than ten years and some
five thousand procedures behind him, are on the site where a patient
weighing competence will look for them, rather than at the top of a page
somebody is reading in pain.

## Which details actually mattered?

- Scroll animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, so patients who turn
  animations off get an instant, still page.
- The smile gallery handles before and after photographs properly, at full
  size, without a third-party lightbox script.
- A sitemap, a robots file and per-page metadata were part of launch rather
  than something added afterwards.
- Three articles answer the questions that come up before booking: the signs
  you need a root canal, root canal against extraction, and what it costs.

## What was in scope, and what was not?

This project covers the website. It does not include the Google Business
Profile, ongoing SEO or monthly care, and we are not going to claim credit
for patient numbers we cannot measure from here.

What you can check yourself: it is live at
[pramukhdentalclinic.com](https://pramukhdentalclinic.com). Open it on your
own phone, on mobile data rather than wifi, and time how long it takes
before you can read something.
