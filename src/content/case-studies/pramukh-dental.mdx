## What the clinic needed

Pramukh Multispeciality Dental Clinic serves patients at Radhanpur Circle in
Mehsana. Like most good clinics, its reputation was built in person, one
treatment at a time. The job was to make the website carry the same
impression to somebody who has not met the doctor yet, and who is deciding
between three clinics on a phone.

That framing decided everything else. This is not a brochure for people who
already chose the practice. It is written for the patient who has not.

## The details that matter

- Scroll animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, so patients who turn
  animations off get an instant, still page.
- The smile gallery handles before and after photographs properly, at full
  size, without a third-party lightbox script.
- A sitemap, a robots file and per-page metadata were part of launch rather
  than something added afterwards.
- Three articles answer the questions that come up before booking: the signs
  you need a root canal, root canal against extraction, and what it costs.

## Honest scope

This project covers the website. It does not include the Google Business
Profile, ongoing SEO or monthly care, and we are not going to claim credit
for patient numbers we cannot measure from here.

What you can check yourself: it is live at
[pramukhdentalclinic.com](https://pramukhdentalclinic.com). Open it on your
