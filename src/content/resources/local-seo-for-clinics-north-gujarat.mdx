When someone in Mehsana searches "skin doctor near me", Google makes a
judgment: which nearby clinics most deserve to be shown? Local SEO is the
work of deserving it — and being legible enough for Google to notice.

This is a plain-language tour of how that judgment works, written for
clinic owners, not marketers.

## What decides which clinics appear in local search?

For local searches, Google weighs three broad things:

**Relevance.** Does your online presence clearly say you offer what was
searched? A clinic with a dedicated, well-written page about a treatment
is more relevant for that search than one with a services list.

**Distance.** How close you are to the searcher. You cannot change this —
which is exactly why the factors you *can* change matter so much within
your area.

**Prominence.** How established you look: reviews and ratings, how
complete and active your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile)
is, mentions of your practice across the web, and whether your details are
consistent everywhere.

## The groundwork you can control

### One truth, everywhere

Your name, address and phone number must be identical on your website,
your Google listing, and every directory that mentions you. Every mismatch
— an old number on Justdial, "Rd" versus "Road" — costs a little
confidence. Auditing and fixing these citations is unglamorous and
effective.

### Pages that answer real searches

Search demand in North Gujarat is specific: treatment plus place.
"Physiotherapy Visnagar", "dental implant cost Mehsana", "child specialist
Palanpur". Each specific search deserves a genuine page — not a
copy-pasted page with the city name swapped (Google detects and demotes
those), but real content that serves that patient.

### Structured data

Schema markup is a machine-readable label on your pages: *this is a
medical clinic, at this address, offering these services, with these
FAQs.* It helps search engines — and increasingly AI assistants — cite you
confidently. Most clinic websites in the region have none, which makes it
a quiet advantage.

### Speed and mobile experience

Google measures how real users experience your site (its Core Web Vitals),
and slow sites rank worse. Since nearly all patient searches here happen
on phones, [speed is both a ranking factor and a first
impression](/resources/website-speed-and-patient-trust).

## SEO tactics that backfire

Paying for "guaranteed #1 ranking" (nobody controls that), buying
backlinks or reviews (penalized), stuffing city names into every sentence
(demoted, and it reads terribly to the patients you are trying to
impress). If a tactic feels like a trick, Google has usually already
built the detector.

## How long does local SEO take?

Local SEO compounds slowly: indexing within weeks, movement over months,
durable visibility over quarters. That pace is a feature — it is why the
