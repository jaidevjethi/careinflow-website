## What is a Google Business Profile, and why does it decide who calls you?

Search your own clinic's name tonight. The panel that appears, the
rating, the photos, the opening hours, is the first impression most
patients get. Most practices have never deliberately managed it.

Google Business Profile management means treating that listing like your
reception desk: accurate, current, and answering before the patient has to
ask.

## What does a patient check in ten seconds?

Patients scan a listing in a fixed order. Rating first. Then photos. Then
"open now". Then distance.

A missing photo set or wrong Sunday hours quietly sends them to the clinic
listed below you. We rebuild the profile properly, correct categories,
complete services, real photographs, holiday hours, and then keep it that
way, month after month.

## How should a clinic handle Google reviews?

Reviews decide the first cut, and they are where practices most often go
wrong. Bought reviews are obvious, and Google removes them.

Our approach is slower and better:

- Make it easy for genuinely happy patients to leave a review.
- Reply to every review, professionally and promptly.
- Never confirm anyone's treatment details in a reply, patient privacy
  comes first, always.

We draft the replies. You approve them.
