## The homepage you did not design

Search your own clinic's name tonight. What comes up — the rating, the
photos, the opening hours — is the first impression most patients get, and
most practices have never touched it.

Google Business Profile management means treating that listing with the
same professionalism as your reception: accurate, current, and answering
before the patient has to ask.

## What a patient checks in ten seconds

Patients scan a listing in a fixed order: rating, then photos, then "open
now", then distance. A missing photo set or wrong Sunday hours quietly
sends them to the clinic listed below you.

We rebuild the profile properly — correct categories, complete services,
real photographs, holiday hours — and then keep it that way, month after
month.

## Reviews, handled honestly

Reviews are the closest thing local search has to a currency, and they are
also where practices most often go wrong. Bought reviews are obvious, and
Google removes them along with your credibility.

Our approach is slower and better: make it effortless for genuinely happy
patients to leave a review, and reply to every one — professionally,
promptly, and within the limits patient privacy requires. We draft the
replies; you approve them.

## One truth, everywhere

Google cross-checks your listing against your website and the rest of the
web. Mismatched phone numbers or addresses cost visibility. Because we also
build [clinic websites](/services/healthcare-websites) and do the
[local SEO groundwork](/services/local-seo), your details stay identical on
every surface — one name, one address, one phone number.
