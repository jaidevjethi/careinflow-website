## What does managed actually mean?

Search your own clinic's name tonight. The panel that appears, the rating,
the photos, the opening hours, is the first impression most patients get,
and for most practices nobody owns it.

We do. Managed means the listing is somebody's job on a schedule rather
than something remembered twice a year: categories and services rebuilt
properly at the start, then hours, holidays, photos, questions and review
replies kept current every month, with your details matched to your
website so Google never sees two versions of you.

If what you want first is to understand the thing rather than hand it
over, our guide to
[Google Business Profile for doctors and clinics](/resources/google-business-profile-for-clinics/)
covers claiming, verification, categories and reviews in full, and you are
welcome to do all of it yourself.

## What does a patient check in ten seconds?

Patients scan a listing in a fixed order. Rating first. Then photos. Then
"open now". Then distance.

A missing photo set or wrong Sunday hours quietly sends them to the clinic
listed below you. We rebuild the profile properly, correct categories,
complete services, real photographs, holiday hours, and then keep it that
way, month after month.

## Who writes the review replies?

We draft them. You approve them. Nothing is published in your clinic's
voice without a doctor reading it first, which matters more here than in
any other industry we could have chosen to work in.

The reason this is a service rather than advice is that replying is
relentless. A review arrives on a Sunday, the useful reply is the one
posted on Sunday, and the practice that answers every time reads
differently from the practice that answered three times in March. We watch
the feed so you are not checking it between patients.

Two things we will not do at any price: buy reviews, or confirm anything
about a patient's treatment in a public reply. The first gets detected and
removed along with your credibility. The second is a privacy breach with
your name on it.

## What happens in the first month?

Week one is the audit, and you get it in writing whether or not you go
ahead: categories, services, hours, photos, review handling, and how your
details compare with your website and the directories carrying your name.

Weeks two and three are the rebuild. Categories corrected, services listed
properly, real photographs uploaded, holiday hours entered ahead of the
festival calendar rather than after it, and every unanswered question and
review cleared.

From week four it is maintenance, and the point of maintenance is that
nothing dramatic happens again. Because we also build
[clinic websites](/services/healthcare-websites/) and do the
[local SEO groundwork](/services/local-seo/), your name, address and phone
number stay identical on every surface Google checks.

You own the profile throughout. It is registered to your Google account,
not ours, and if you stop the plan you keep everything.
