## What a clinic website is actually for

A patient who has never met you decides in about a minute whether your
practice feels trustworthy. That decision usually happens on a phone, at
night, often after a search like "dentist near me" or "skin doctor in
Mehsana".

Your website's job is simple: look professional, answer the patient's real
questions, and make the next step obvious. Everything we build serves those
three things.

## Written for a slightly worried patient

People searching for a doctor are rarely relaxed. They want to know what a
treatment involves, what it costs, how long it takes, and whether it will
hurt. Most clinic websites answer none of this.

We write a page for every treatment you offer, in the words patients
actually type — symptom and cost, not clinical terminology. That is also
exactly the content Google and AI assistants reward, which is why our
websites and our [local SEO work](/services/local-seo) are designed
together.

## Fast on the phones patients actually own

Speed is not a technical nicety. A page that takes six seconds to load on a
₹12,000 Android loses the patient before it says a word.

We build with almost no JavaScript, optimize every image, and measure the
result on a mid-range Android over mobile data. The reference we hold
ourselves to is a largest-contentful-paint under 1.2 seconds — and this very
website is built the same way, so you can judge the claim yourself.

## The enquiry flow, done properly

A beautiful site that hides its phone number is a brochure, not a tool.
We make contact effortless: tap-to-call, tap-for-directions, and a WhatsApp
button that opens a pre-written message so the patient only has to press
send. Enquiries route to wherever your desk will actually answer them.

## What happens after launch

A website is not finished at launch — hours change, treatments are added,
software needs updating. That is why every build includes the option of
[ongoing website care](/services/website-care), with a written maintenance
schedule instead of vague promises.

## Where we work

We work from one studio in Mehsana and build for practices across
Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Visnagar, Patan and the wider North Gujarat
region. Being local matters: we can visit your practice, photograph it
properly, and understand who your patients are.
