## What should a clinic website do for your practice?

A patient who has never met you decides in about a minute whether your
clinic looks trustworthy. That decision usually happens on a phone, at
night, after a search like "dentist near me" or "skin doctor in Mehsana".

So your website has three jobs. Look professional. Answer the questions
the patient actually has. Make the next step obvious. Everything we build
serves those three jobs, and we leave out anything that does not.

## Why every treatment needs its own page

Most clinic websites have one "Services" page that lists everything in a
few lines. That page cannot rank for any specific search, and it cannot
answer any specific worry.

We write a page for each treatment you offer. Each one covers what the
treatment involves, how long it takes, what affects the cost, and what
recovery feels like — in the words patients type, not clinical terms.
That is also the content Google and AI assistants reward, which is why
our website work and our [local SEO work](/services/local-seo) are planned
together.

## How fast should a clinic website load?

Fast enough that nobody leaves. A page that takes six seconds on a ₹12,000
Android loses the patient before it says a word.

We use almost no JavaScript, we compress every image, and we measure the
result on a mid-range Android over mobile data. Our reference is a largest
contentful paint under 1.2 seconds. This website is built the same way, so
you can test the claim on your own phone right now.

## How patients contact you

A beautiful site that hides its phone number is a brochure, not a tool.

We make contact effortless: tap to call, tap for directions, and a
WhatsApp button that opens a message the patient only has to send. Those
enquiries go wherever your front desk will actually see them.

## What happens after the website launches?

Hours change. New doctors join. Software needs updating. That is why every
build can continue into [ongoing website care](/services/website-care),
with a written schedule instead of a vague promise.

## Which cities do you build for?

We work from one studio in Mehsana and build for practices across
Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Visnagar, Unjha, Patan and the wider North Gujarat
region. Being close helps: we can visit your clinic, photograph it
properly, and understand who your patients are.
