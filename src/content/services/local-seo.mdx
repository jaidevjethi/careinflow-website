## How do patients find a clinic near them?

Almost every new patient starts with a search: a symptom, a treatment, or
"near me". The clinics that appear are not always the best doctors. They
are the ones whose groundwork is done.

Local SEO is that groundwork. Not tricks, pages that genuinely answer
what patients ask, on a site search engines can read easily.

## What decides which clinics appear in local search?

Google weighs three things.

**Relevance.** Does your site clearly show you offer what was searched? A
clinic with a proper page about a treatment beats one with a services
list.

**Distance.** How close you are to the searcher. You cannot change this,
which is exactly why the other two matter so much.

**Prominence.** How established you look: reviews, a complete
[Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile), mentions
across the web, and consistent details everywhere.

## Why is one “Services” page not enough?

Search demand here is specific: "physiotherapy Visnagar", "dental implant
cost Mehsana", "child specialist Palanpur".

We map what patients in your area actually search for, then build the
coverage: a real page for each treatment, and honest location context for
each area you serve. Not the same page with the city name swapped, Google
detects that and demotes it.

## What technical work do patients never see?

- Clean, semantic page structure and correct headings
- Schema markup for your practice, services and FAQs, validated
- Fast pages that pass Google's Core Web Vitals
- An XML sitemap, correct canonical URLs, no broken links
- Google Search Console and analytics, set up and handed to you

## What is the answer Google writes above the results?

Search "best dental clinic in Mehsana" today and Google will often write you
an answer before the first link. It assembles that answer from pages it can
read and quote, which means a patient can decide before reaching anybody's
website at all.

So we make sure your practice's facts are published, consistent and easy to
quote: what you treat, how you price it, who the doctor is, and the questions
patients actually ask. We cannot promise Google will quote you. What we can
say is that a practice whose facts are nowhere will not be quoted, and almost
no clinic in India has published them yet.

## What will we not do for local SEO?

No bought backlinks. No doorway pages. No hidden text. No city names
stuffed into every sentence. No ranking guarantees. These tricks buy
short-term movement and long-term penalties, and we do not sell them.
