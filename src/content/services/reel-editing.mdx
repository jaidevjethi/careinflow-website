## What does a doctor send?

- One video, recorded on your phone: upright, in a quiet room, with your face and voice clear
- The topic, and the one patient question it answers
- Gujarati or English for the captions
- Your logo and contact details, once
- What patients should do next: call, send a WhatsApp message, or book

One question per video. "Why do my gums bleed when I brush?" makes a better
reel than "Everything about dental health", and it is easier to record.

## Gujarati or English?

Whichever your patients read. Gujarati when you speak Gujarati on camera and
your patients do too. English when they are younger or more urban, or when the
treatment names are English words anyway. Gujarati with the key term kept in
English when both matter. The [healthcare content page](/services/social-media/)
says more about choosing.

## Patient explainers and testimonials

Most reels we edit are one of two kinds.

**Patient explainers.** A doctor answering a question patients ask in the
chair: what a treatment involves, when a symptom needs attention, what recovery
actually feels like.

**Patient testimonials.** A patient describing their experience in their own
words, where your council's rules allow it and only with their written consent
to appear and to be shared. The National Medical Commission's advertising
guidelines of 6 October 2026 do not allow registered medical practitioners to
use patient testimonials to promote their practice on social media, so we check
which rules apply to you before we edit one. Where one is allowed, we edit for
clarity, never for claims: we do not add words the patient did not say, suggest
a treatment works for everyone, or show anything that identifies them beyond
what they agreed to. More on this is on the
[healthcare content page](/services/social-media/).

## Revisions and turnaround

One revision is included. Changes beyond that, or a re-edit to a different
brief, are quoted before we make them.

A standard reel is usually ready in one to two working days from the footage
arriving. Same-day turnaround is not something we promise.

## What we will not do

- Promise reach, followers, enquiries or patients. Nobody honestly can.
- Add a medical claim you did not make, or one that cannot be supported
- Post on your account. You publish it, once you have approved it
- Use a patient's face, name or story without their written consent
