## Where social media fits — and where it does not

Social media is a **support service** at CareInflow. It is not one of the
things we lead with, and we will not sell it to a practice whose website
or Google listing still needs work. Those come first, every time, because
that is where patients actually decide.

What social media does well is confirm. A patient who found you through
search often checks your profile before calling. If the last post is from
two years ago, that hesitates them. If it shows a practice that explains
things clearly and posts steadily, it settles them.

## What we post for a healthcare practice

The best clinic content is the answer to a question you already give ten
times a week in the chair:

- What a treatment involves, step by step
- What recovery actually feels like, honestly
- When a symptom means "come in today"
- What affects the cost of a procedure
- Practical clinic updates — new equipment, festival hours, a new doctor

We write these as short, plain-language posts. You approve everything
before it goes out, and you correct anything clinically inaccurate.

## Patient privacy comes before content

Healthcare social media has limits that other industries do not. We hold
to them even when it costs reach:

- No clinical photographs without clear written consent
- No patient stories that could identify someone who has not agreed
- No replies that confirm whether a person was treated at your clinic
- No claims about outcomes we cannot support

A post that embarrasses a patient is worse for your practice than no post
at all.

## What we do not do

We do not run paid advertising, manage ad budgets, chase follower counts,
or buy engagement. We do not promise that social media will fill your
appointment book. If someone promises you that, ask them how they will
measure it.

## Start with the foundation

If you are weighing social media, the honest first step is to check
whether the two surfaces that matter more are right: your
[clinic website](/services/healthcare-websites) and your
[Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile). The free
review covers both, and tells you plainly where social media sits on your
list — which is sometimes near the bottom.
