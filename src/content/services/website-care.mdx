## What happens to your website after launch?

Most clinic websites are abandoned the day they go live, by the agency
that built them. Two years later the doctor list is wrong, the copyright
says the wrong year, and the enquiry button quietly stopped working.

Website Care is our answer to the question clinics rarely think to ask, and
we answer it in writing.

## Why is it priced to your site?

A five-page site that changes twice a year and a thirty-page site with a new
doctor every season are not the same month of work. Publishing one figure for
both would mean inventing it. So Website Care is a custom monthly plan: we look
at your site in the [free written review](/contact/), then put one price in
writing, and it does not move unless the scope does.

## Do you need it if you have Local SEO & Google Care?

Usually not. [Local SEO & Google Care](/services/local-seo/), at
{rupees(GOOGLE_CARE.monthly)} a month, already includes changes to your website,
speed checks and a new treatment page every month, alongside the Google listing
and local search work. Website Care is for a practice that wants its website
looked after without the Google side. If your site needs more technical upkeep
than that, the free review will say so.

## How quickly are small changes made?

A new doctor joins. Diwali hours change. You start offering a treatment
that deserves its own page.

These small updates are where most websites rot, because asking the
original developer feels like starting a project. With care in place, you
send a WhatsApp message and it gets done.

## Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, hosting, code, content, analytics and search accounts
are registered in your practice's name from day one.

If we ever part ways, you lose nothing. We think that is the only honest
way to run maintenance, and it is written into every agreement.

## How do we prove the site still works?

We publish the standards we hold ourselves to: load time references,
accessibility and validated schema, and this website is held to the same ones.

begins.
