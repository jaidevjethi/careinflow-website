## Launch is the start, not the end

Most clinic websites are abandoned the day they go live — by the agency
that built them. Two years later the copyright says the wrong year, the
doctor list is outdated, and the contact form quietly stopped working.

Ongoing care is our answer to the question clinics rarely think to ask:
*"what happens after launch?"* It is the objection almost no competitor
answers, so we answer it in writing.

## A schedule, not a promise

"We'll take care of it" is not a plan. This is:

**Weekly** — uptime and performance monitoring, review activity checked on
your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile).

**Monthly** — security updates, off-site backups, content edits you have
asked for, profile photos and hours refreshed.

**Quarterly** — speed re-measured against its reference on a real phone, a
written review of what patients did, what changed nearby, and what we
recommend next.

## Small changes, done promptly

A new doctor joins. Diwali hours change. You start offering a treatment
that deserves its own page. These small updates are where most websites rot,
because asking the original developer feels like starting a project.

With care in place, you message us on WhatsApp and it gets done — usually
within days, without a new quotation for every sentence.

## Your site stays yours

Everything is registered in your name — domain, hosting, analytics, search
accounts. If we ever part ways, you lose nothing. We think that is the only
honest way to run maintenance, and it is written into every agreement.

## Built to be measured

We publish the standards we hold ourselves to — load time references,
accessibility, validated schema — and this website runs on the same care
schedule we sell. The [method](/process) page explains how the first four
weeks work before care begins.
